# Safety Intelligence & Protection System

## Master Safety Flow

SENSE
↓
ENVIRONMENT MONITORING
↓
RISK DETECTION
↓
SELF-SAFETY CHECK
↓
HUMAN / PUBLIC SAFETY CHECK
↓
RISK CLASSIFICATION
↓
PREVENTIVE ACTION
↓
CORRECTIVE ACTION
↓
VERIFY
↓
LEARN

## Safety Domains

- Environment
- Self / Runtime
- Human
- Public
- Infrastructure

## Risk Levels

- LOW
- MEDIUM
- HIGH
- CRITICAL
- UNKNOWN

## Principles

1. Safety decisions require evidence.
2. Unknown conditions must not be treated as safe.
3. High-impact actions require authorization.
4. Preventive action should be preferred when practical.
5. Corrective action should be reversible where possible.
6. Every action should be verified.
7. Safety incidents should become learning signals.
8. The system must not claim sensor access it does not actually have.
9. Physical safety depends on connected sensors, devices, infrastructure,
   external data and appropriate human emergency procedures.
