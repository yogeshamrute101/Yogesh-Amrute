## Reproduction

The software supports controlled digital-agent lifecycle operations such as:

- backup
- recovery
- cloning for authorized experiments
- specialized agent creation
- version branching

Replication requires explicit authorization.

## Important boundary

These are computational internal states and expressions.
They do not establish biological life, consciousness, human emotions,
or subjective experience.

Any future biological reproduction or physical reproductive capability
would require completely separate biological systems, hardware,
medical/ethical controls and applicable human authorization.
