## MasterMind responsibilities

1. Coordinate all intelligence modules.
2. Maintain the current reasoning cycle.
3. Combine perception, memory, decisions and future planning.
4. Track objectives.
5. Select next steps.
6. Detect uncertainty.
7. Route high-impact actions for approval.
8. Verify results.
9. Feed verified outcomes back into aggregation/memory.
10. Re-evaluate plans when new evidence appears.

## Important principle

MasterMind does not mean consciousness.

It is the software's central reasoning and orchestration layer.

No module should silently claim certainty when evidence is insufficient.
