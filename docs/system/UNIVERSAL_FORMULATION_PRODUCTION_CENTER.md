# Universal Formulation & Production Center

## Purpose

Provide one common architecture for:

- formulas
- formulations
- recipes
- product specifications
- material/component selection
- process design
- manufacturing planning
- quality control
- validation
- scale-up
- production monitoring

## Formula intelligence

Formula records contain:

- equation
- variables
- units
- assumptions
- validity domain
- validation status

The system must not treat an unverified formula as production-ready.

## Manufacturing intelligence

Manufacturing plans can contain:

- process steps
- inputs
- outputs
- process parameters
- equipment
- controls
- hazards
- quality gates
- safety gates

## Important safety boundary

Different domains require fundamentally different scientific and regulatory
models.

Pharmaceuticals, chemicals, semiconductor fabrication, vehicles, aerospace,
electrical systems and other safety-critical products require validated domain
models, standards, testing and qualified professional review.

The architecture does not claim that a generic engine can safely invent or
validate every real-world production recipe from first principles.

Restricted or hazardous manufacturing remains subject to the existing safety,
authorization and governance layers.
