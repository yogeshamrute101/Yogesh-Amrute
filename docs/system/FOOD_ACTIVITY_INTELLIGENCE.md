# Food & Activity Intelligence

## Food capabilities

- Meal planning
- Recipe generation
- Ingredient analysis
- Nutrition analysis
- Recipe adaptation
- Cooking guidance
- Food safety
- Storage guidance

## Human activity capabilities

- Activity observation
- Activity classification
- Exercise planning
- Routine planning
- Activity tracking
- Rest/recovery planning
- Accessibility-aware activity guidance

## Animal activity capabilities

- Species-aware activity planning
- Behavior observation
- Enrichment planning
- Feeding-activity coordination
- Activity tracking
- Environment-aware suggestions

Animal activity must respect species, age, environment, welfare and safety.
The system must not assume that a human activity is appropriate for an animal.

## Physical execution boundary

The intelligence layer can plan and guide activities.

Actual cooking, feeding, exercise, movement, machine operation or physical
interaction requires the appropriate human, robot, appliance, sensor or other
authorized physical interface.

## Safety

Food advice must account for allergies, toxicity, contamination and relevant
dietary restrictions.

Animal guidance must account for species-specific hazards and welfare.

Medical or nutritional conditions should be referred to qualified
professionals when the situation is high-risk or uncertain.
