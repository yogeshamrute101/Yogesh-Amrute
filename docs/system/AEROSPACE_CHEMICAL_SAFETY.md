# Aerospace & Chemical Safety Engineering

Provides safe engineering intelligence for aerospace research,
propulsion theory, hypersonic-flow simulation, thermodynamics,
materials, controls, structural analysis, testing and verification.

Provides chemical safety intelligence for hazard analysis, storage,
handling, compatibility, process safety, waste management and
regulatory documentation.

Weapon construction, explosive design, chemical-weapon development,
and harmful-agent synthesis are explicitly outside the system scope.

Safety-critical physical work requires qualified professionals,
validated tools, controlled facilities and appropriate authorization.
