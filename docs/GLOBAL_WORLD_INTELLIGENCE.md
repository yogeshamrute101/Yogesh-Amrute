## Maturity Principles

1. Do not act before understanding context.
2. Separate evidence from inference.
3. Track uncertainty explicitly.
4. Consider consequences before high-impact actions.
5. Prefer reversible actions when uncertainty is high.
6. Continuously update the world model.
7. Never claim knowledge or sensor access that is not actually available.
8. External data sources, sensors and research are required for real-world
   coverage and current information.
9. The system should learn from verified observations and outcomes.
