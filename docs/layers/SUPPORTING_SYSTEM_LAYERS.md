# Supporting System Layers

The architecture now includes supporting layers for:

1. Identity and Context
2. Truth and Uncertainty
3. Privacy and Data Protection
4. Security
5. Testing and Quality Assurance
6. Performance and Cost
7. Versioning and Change Management
8. Disaster Recovery
9. Communication
10. UX and Accessibility
11. Localization
12. Policy and Ethics
13. Experimentation
14. Dependency Management
15. Deployment and Infrastructure

These are frameworks for controlled system behavior. They do not automatically
grant access to external accounts, devices, services, networks, cloud systems,
physical systems, or private data.
