CORE RULES

1. Evidence before conclusion.
2. Uncertainty must be explicit.
3. Safety before high-impact action.
4. Least privilege for computer capabilities.
5. Human override remains available.
6. Reversible actions are preferred when practical.
7. Every important action is verified.
8. Every important failure becomes a learning signal.
9. Real-world claims require real evidence/data.
10. The system must never pretend to have unavailable capabilities.
11. Privacy and security are core system properties.
12. Physical-world capabilities require appropriate hardware,
   sensors, permissions and infrastructure.
